### Event Objectives

- Help students gain a better understanding of the latest trends in Cloud Computing and Generative AI within modern enterprises.
- Provide insights into how organizations leverage Cloud technologies to build and operate large-scale systems.
- Introduce career pathways and job opportunities in the Cloud Computing industry.
- Create opportunities for students to interact with and learn from industry experts.

### Event Overview

The Study Tour featured speakers from Amazon Web Services (AWS) Vietnam, Renova Cloud, Cloud Kinetics, and experienced engineers specializing in Generative AI. Throughout the event, participants explored a variety of practical topics related to Cloud Computing and enterprise system architecture.

The main topics included:

- An overview of the Cloud Computing market and future career opportunities.
- The role of Cloud Computing and Generative AI in digital transformation.
- Career-sharing sessions from data engineers on their learning and professional journeys.
- How enterprises design and optimize data architectures using Cloud technologies.
- A panel discussion on developing the next generation of technology professionals in Vietnam.

### Knowledge and Skills Acquired

#### Technical Knowledge

- Gained a deeper understanding of the role of Cloud Computing in modern enterprise systems.
- Learned how organizations use AWS to store, process, and manage data at scale.
- Expanded my knowledge of Generative AI and its integration with Cloud platforms.
- Better understood the importance of system architecture in ensuring performance, scalability, and high availability.

#### Career Development

- Learned about the current demand for Cloud Computing professionals in the job market.
- Gained a clearer understanding of the career path toward becoming a Cloud Engineer or Data Engineer.
- Recognized the importance of continuous learning and keeping up with emerging technologies.

### Topics Covered

- Fundamental and advanced concepts of Cloud Computing.
- Enterprise system architecture design and best practices.
- Practical applications of Cloud technologies in real-world business environments.

### Experience During the Event

- The presentations provided valuable insights from experienced professionals working in the Cloud Computing industry.
- Real-world case studies helped me better understand how AWS solutions are implemented in enterprise environments.
- The career-sharing sessions provided useful guidance for developing my technical skills and planning my future career.
- The panel discussion offered different perspectives on technology trends and the growing demand for Cloud professionals.

### Lessons Learned

- A strong theoretical foundation is essential, but the ability to apply knowledge to real-world scenarios is even more important.
- Cloud Computing and Generative AI continue to create exciting career opportunities while requiring continuous learning and skill development.
- Besides technical expertise, communication, teamwork, and problem-solving skills are equally important for technology professionals.
- Taking initiative in learning, practicing, and participating in technical events is an effective way to improve professional competence and adapt to the rapidly evolving technology industry.
