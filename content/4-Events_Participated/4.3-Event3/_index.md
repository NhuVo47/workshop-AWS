### Event Objectives

- Gain an overview of Amazon Bedrock AgentCore and its core components.
- Learn the process of building and deploying AI Agents on AWS.
- Gain hands-on experience in deploying a basic AI Agent and integrating it with AWS services.
- Enhance knowledge of user authentication and integration with external services.

### Event Overview

The workshop consisted of two main sessions: a technical presentation and a hands-on lab.

#### Presentation Session

During the first session, the speakers introduced the fundamentals of Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, including:

- An overview of Amazon Bedrock AgentCore.
- The role of Runtime in AI Agent execution.
- How Gateway facilitates communication between users and AI Agents.
- Identity management and user authentication mechanisms.
- The overall architecture of AgentCore and its key components.

#### Hands-on Lab Session

During the hands-on lab, I completed the practical exercises under the guidance of the instructors, including:

- Deploying a basic AI Agent using Amazon Bedrock AgentCore.
- Connecting the Agent to external tools and knowledge bases.
- Building a web interface for interacting with the Agent.
- Integrating user authentication with Amazon Cognito.
- Testing and validating the application after completing the configuration.

### Knowledge and Skills Acquired

#### Technical Knowledge

- Gained an understanding of the architecture and core components of Amazon Bedrock AgentCore.
- Learned the process of building and deploying AI Agents on AWS.
- Understood how AI Agents can leverage external tools and knowledge bases to perform tasks.
- Learned the role of Amazon Cognito in identity management and user authentication.

#### Practical Skills

- Became familiar with the AWS environment for deploying AI Agents.
- Configured and deployed an AI Agent by following the workshop instructions.
- Integrated multiple AWS services to build a complete AI-powered application.
- Observed and tested the deployment workflow of an AI Agent application.

### Experience During the Workshop

- The combination of theoretical presentations and hands-on practice made it easier to understand and apply the concepts throughout the workshop.
- The step-by-step lab exercises allowed me to deploy an AI Agent and gain a deeper understanding of how Amazon Bedrock AgentCore works.
- Configuring the system components myself helped me better understand the process of developing AI Agents on AWS.
- I also had the opportunity to interact with the speakers and other participants whenever I encountered difficulties during the lab sessions.

### Lessons Learned

- Amazon Bedrock AgentCore provides a powerful platform that simplifies AI Agent development and deployment.
- Integrating AI Agents with knowledge bases and AWS services enables the development of scalable and manageable AI applications.
- Hands-on practice reinforced the theoretical concepts presented during the workshop.
- The workshop provided valuable practical experience in building AI applications on AWS and motivated me to further explore AI services and technologies in the future.
