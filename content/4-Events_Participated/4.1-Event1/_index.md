### Event Objectives

- Provide an academic environment for participants to review and strengthen their Cloud Computing knowledge.
- Develop analytical thinking and system architecture design skills through real-world scenarios.
- Encourage collaboration, knowledge sharing, and teamwork among participants.
- Enhance the ability to apply Cloud knowledge to solve problems ranging from basic to advanced levels.

### Event Format

- The event was organized as a team-based competition.
- Each match consisted of two teams competing against each other.
- Teams answered a series of questions with increasing levels of difficulty.
- After all questions were completed, the team with the higher score advanced to the next round.
- In the event of a tie, an additional question was used to determine the winner.

### Participation Rules

- Total number of teams: 8.
- Each team consisted of 5 members.
- Team members could come from different internship groups.
- Each participant was allowed to join only one team.

### Special Game Features

#### Minimum Risk (One-Time Use)

- Used when the team was unsure about the answer.
- If the answer was correct, the team received 50% of the question's score.
- If the answer was incorrect, no points were deducted.

#### Star of Hope (One-Time Use)

- Used for the question the team felt most confident about.
- A correct answer doubled the score.
- An incorrect answer resulted in double point deduction.

### Knowledge Covered

The questions focused on Cloud Computing and system architecture design. The content ranged from fundamental concepts to scenario-based questions that required participants to analyze problems and choose appropriate architectural solutions.

### Knowledge and Skills Gained

#### Cloud Computing and System Architecture

- Reinforced fundamental concepts of Cloud Computing.
- Improved understanding of how to design and evaluate system architectures.
- Gained experience in approaching real-world problems from the perspective of a Cloud Architect.

#### Teamwork Skills

- Improved communication and collaboration among team members.
- Learned how to assign responsibilities effectively during discussions.
- Enhanced the ability to reach decisions within a limited time.

#### Strategic Thinking

- Learned to choose the appropriate time to use special game features.
- Evaluated potential risks before making decisions.
- Developed strategies to maximize the team's overall score.

### Practical Applications

- Strengthened Cloud Computing knowledge for certifications such as AWS and Microsoft Azure.
- Improved system design thinking for real-world software projects.
- Enhanced analytical skills for solving System Design interview questions.
- Applied architectural thinking to backend development and distributed systems.

### Experience During the Event

- The competitive atmosphere made the event both engaging and motivating while encouraging teamwork.
- The questions required not only theoretical knowledge but also analytical thinking to identify the most suitable solutions.
- Several architecture-related scenarios provided a clearer understanding of how Cloud solutions are designed and implemented in practice.
- The use of special game features encouraged careful consideration before making decisions in order to maximize the team's performance.
- Collaborating with teammates under time constraints helped improve my communication, discussion, and coordination skills.

### Lessons Learned

- I realized that understanding the overall system architecture is just as important as understanding individual Cloud services.
- Every architectural decision should consider multiple factors, including performance, scalability, cost, and reliability.
- Analyzing requirements before selecting a solution helps avoid inappropriate architectural designs.
- In addition to technical knowledge, teamwork, critical thinking, and quick decision-making are essential skills when solving real-world engineering problems.
